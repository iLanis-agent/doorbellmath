Doorbell math

The battery doorbell dies two days before the package arrives, and nobody knows if the old doorbell transformer in the wall can carry a video bell. Doorbell math turns the pack, the rings and the standby drain into days between charges, reads the transformer rating against the bell and chime draw for a labeled headroom margin, and prices charging against wiring in real money. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Battery plan - capacity, rings and standby drain become days between charges.
  • Transformer plan - transformer VA versus bell and chime draw becomes a labeled headroom margin.
  • Power plan - charging the pack versus a wired bell, priced in cents a year.

Labeled norms

Labeled pack: 6000 mAh at 80% usable. Labeled ring: 300 mA for 20 seconds, 8 rings a day, 2.5 mA standby. Labeled transformer: 10 VA. Labeled grid price: 15 cents a kWh. These are reference values, not specs - the headroom verdict is a planning estimate, and the bell maker's rating and a qualified electrician decide what is safe to wire.
